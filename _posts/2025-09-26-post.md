Picked up some final extras in Raglan, packed everything up on the bike, and left the farm at 14:00.

Beautiful weather as I set off which stuck around for a long while as I took in some beautiful vistas of jagged coast and estuary. Eventually made it to Nikau Caves Café, drenched. The heavens decided to open 20 minutes before I arrived. 

Met Philip, the sheep shearer. He and his family hold a surprising number of sheep shearing world records! His and Anne's hospitality was amazing: they fed me scrambled egg (with cheese and parsley), beer and cake!

Legs are very tired on account of a heavy bike, a stubborn headwind, and far too many hills!
