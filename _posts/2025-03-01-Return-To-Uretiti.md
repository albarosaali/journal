We departed Trounson at 09:30 to see Tane Mahute. The biggest Kauri tree in the world. It is enormous! It looms above the canopy, and down over you, in a sort of paternal way, at least, it made me feed at ease. We proceeded then to see the second biggest Kauri tree which looked to be even greater in diameter but comparatively stunted height-wise; we appreciated the atmospheric walkway leading up to it.

Quick stop made at the Kauri gum shop, we set off for Waipu Cove which we drove through a week ago. Stopped once at a café, Tam relish purchased. Welcomed with gorgeous beach and sunshine at Waipu Cove. Remnants of beetroot curry devoured. Refreshing lager consumed at beachside restaurant/bar to accompany some trip admin. Maddy and I recommended for jobs there by a friendly waiter from Warrington, my shirt did the trick apparently.

Spot acquired in Uretiti in view of Mangawhai heads, 2 nights booked. Ready for some rest.
