Hot shower. Trip to the op-shop - acquired a new pan, organiser, peeler and cushion. Said goodbye to Napier around mid-day though I hope we go back!

Scar Tissue, the audiobook, kept us enthralled on the almost 3 hour drive to Taupo, including a stop at the colossal Waipunga Falls lookout.

On arrival in Taupo, post devouring of a hangry late lunch, we decided to go on a 3 hour ramble to and from Huka falls: a waterfall on the Waikato river, fed by Lake Taupo, through which 200,000 litres of water cascades each second! The speed and turbulence of the flow is quite remarkable! 

Camp made at Reid's park. Maddy cooked aubergine burgers, yum!
