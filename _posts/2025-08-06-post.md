Relaxed morning, before heading back to Taranaki for more hiking. Once again a clear day! 

Parked up at Stratford Plateau, in the shadow of the volcano, then set off down to Wilkies Pools: a mesmerising cascade of crystal clear water down curvaceous volcanic rocks. This was followed up by a rapturous Dawson falls, a rather loud and aggressive waterfall.

We took the ridge walk back to the plateau where me met Quentin and Milene, two French campers, who had also decided to brave the mountain in their van.

A stunning and warming chicken thighs and orange con-cock-tion prepared by Maddy, paired with red wine, naturally. This boosted morale enough for us to endure a suspension testing wind storm on the mountain overnight.
