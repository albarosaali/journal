Escaped from plateau early doors after a torrid night of sleep in the van, cloudy and wet. We returned to Lake Rotomanu, made coffees, ate and headed out on the tandem.

We sailed along the coastal path until we reached Paritutu rock, which we stopped to summit for 360° views of New Plymouth.

Our next stop was Okurukuru vineyard, a pleasingly optimistic and spirited enterprise with decent enough wines. No one else had even thought of making wine in that area! We soldiered on to Ōakura where we stopped for lunch (bread cheese and salami! bought some fancy sausages, then turned around and headed back.

A well earned pint or two was had at the Sinking Peak brewery, where we met Daisy, then we had some pork belly and a stout at the Three Sisters. Lush!
