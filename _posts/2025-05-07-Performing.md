Potential for more solar work, something for Marilyn's yoga studio!

Asked at Miller's coffee for a job, grumpy  response received. Also expressed interest at Raglan FoodCo.

Made a 'Wafflemisu'. No lady fingers available so I improvised and made fresh waffles instead, came out well!

Final guitar practice! Sunset surf squeezed in - I have figured out how to pop-up on the chopstick!

Delicious, meaty dinner prepared by Joy and Jonty; the piri-piri chicken was to die for! Tried a traditional South African sausage time: one quarter beef, one quarter pork, one quarter lamb, and the rest made up with calf heart fat, coriander, pepper and chilli; I could get used to it!

Filled with tasty meats it was showtime! I took the 9pm slot, Joel set up mics and we were away! It went well! Met some of the other performers after who were very encouraging; I'll have to do another. Excellent following acts and a grand boogie till close.
