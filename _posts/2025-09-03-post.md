Spurred the family on down to Fishguard to see a tapestry, made to commemorate the defeat of the French in their attempted invasion of Britain via Fishguard in 1797.

 
Walked down to picturesque Lower Fishguard then set off to St. Davids: a bastion of Welsh history and determinism.

Stopped at a cliff-surrounded beach for some golden hour cliff appreciation.

Evening meal enjoyed at Castle Inn (back
in Newport). They served up a particularly tasty Gammon!
