We woke up with the sun this morning, the animal noises had subsided from last night, and promptly set about the morning routine of eating oats, washing and preparing for the day.

The ferry over to Russell was our next port of call, but not before scouting out the craft market at Paihia and buying some magic moisturiser. By 12 we had crossed the water and set about exploring Russell. First we stomped up Flagstaff hill, which we had learnt about yesterday at Waiting, where we took in some spectacular views of the Bay of Islands, then we marched to the local supermarket for a picnic lunch; enjoyed on the beach front of course. 

"No craft fair can be left unexplored". Edmund Hillary - 1906, we took this mantra to heart, and to our wallets, purchasing a fantastic bowl and some beeswax wraps for the van - Maddy even bought a jacket!

To cool ourselves down after our spree, we indulged in a tipple and a semifreddo at the Gables before we boarded our return ship to Paihia. We gave Vanessa a drink, well earned, and made our way onwards: first to Kerikeri for a shop, then to Mangonui for the night.

In Mangonui we visited a pub with a parrot, Aurora, that Generous Jeff had recommended, along with some Dory and chips for dinner.
Lush.
