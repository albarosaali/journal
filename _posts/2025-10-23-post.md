Astonishingly wet ride this morning, thoroughly drenched. I had at least a courtesy tailwind after battling through 20 km of head.

Arrived in Reefton, soaked, and visited the only open café (the town had a morning powercut) where I enjoyed a coffee with Joel and Pia. They were stuck in Reefton due to road closures!

Eventually befriended Chontel (the owner of the cafe) after striking up conversation with her sister Rachel. She is a treasure trove of secret swimming spots around Queenstown! Chontel offered me a room in her accommodation building next door at a heavily discounted price, how generous!

Hung my things to dry and enjoyed a luxurious hot shower, then took advantage of some sunshine and went on a pub tour of the town. Nourish was my favourite. I was given a free blue lagoon! 

Started rewatching Chernobyl, so good!
