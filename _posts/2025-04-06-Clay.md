Early morning start with a good stretch and, of course, some oats. Shower at the nearby hostel, more bread purchased (yesterday's of course). 

Maddy prepared a date for us at a picturesque point overlooking Whale Bay. The activity? Clay and lunch! Despite our most enthusiastic shaping efforts we simply could not bring the clay to hold it's form in the way we wanted. We've ended up with a wonky plant tray. My Maddy-prepared lunch was delicious, fresh sourdough with a cucumber, tomato and tahini salad. Yum! Refreshing and rich!

Some van-admin tater we decided it was time to make face at Joy and Jonty's to meet the others. We are sharing facilities with Jonas and Clara (German woofers) and Nicolas (Argentine carpenter). Turns out that Nicolas likes to experiment with high voltage AC devices from the late 19th and early 20th century at home! 

Leftover gnocchi for dinner.
