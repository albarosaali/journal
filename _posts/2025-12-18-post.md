Didn't wake up feeling the most fresh, but there were of course things to crack on with. Showed Jonty the new solar set-up, he seems pleased which is good!

Drove to Hamilton to fetch some bike boxes for the tandem, some last minute packing. Managed to sell the guitar to Peter too, bonus! After a large round of hugs and goodbyes, Jonty and me hit the road to Auckland airport at around 17:30 for my flight at 22:10. 

A huge, winding, check-in queue was my
welcoming gift to kick things off; almost impossible to navigate with two bikes and two bags. After a couple of gos schmoozing the service desk, they agreed to let me skip the economy queue, directing me instead to the business line... with no one in it. Result! Both bikes accepted as well!

Post security, I enjoyed a rather good salmon and cream cheese bagel and some sushi before boarding. Was given an emergency exit seat too, unlimited legroom!
