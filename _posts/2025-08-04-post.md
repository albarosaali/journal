Set off this morning on the 4 hour drive down to New Plymouth after the final hot shower I suppose we'll have for a while.

First stop made at Otorhanga where a much needed coffee was had. Swiftly carried on to Mokau where we stopped for lunch, a delicious chicken and mushroom pie snaffled with Taranaki providing a majestic backdrop. Final stip made at the Three Sisters: a dramatic rock formation on a black sand beach.

Set up base at Lake Rotomanu unloaded the tandem and went on an adventure up, then down the coastal walkway to end up in New Plymouth. We immediately found an Italian supermarket so I of course bought some salami and Gorgonzola before we settled in as the only customers at "The Three Sisters", a local brewery. Whiled away the evening with the bartender, Tom, over stouts and snacks than made tracks back to the van via a dark sea front ride.
