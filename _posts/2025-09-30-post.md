Left the tent and bike where they stood at 8:20 to go and summit "The Pinnacles" a mountain in the Coromandel.

Track went through some lush greenery, alongside a babbling river. Met some Dutch girls on the way and stopped to take some photos. 

Made it to the summit at 11:20 where I enjoyed a leisurely early lunch in the sun. Headed back down after and made it back to the tent by 14:30.

Went for a cool dip in the river, and read Book 1 of Aristotle's Nicomachean ethics;
lapping up the sunshine while I did. 

Tuna tomato pasta for dinner, yun! Salami to start.

Where next?....
