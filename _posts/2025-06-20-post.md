Morning spent taking Bindi on a walk for Nick. Long wander up and down Ngarunui beach in sunny bliss, she was once again very well behaved. 

Rest of the day spent rewiring the workshop to accommodate the new switchboard. Finished just as the light began to fade, just in time!

Evening spent down at the old school arts centre for a community gathering on Matariki (a NZ public holiday) with Chai and dinner on offer. Very relaxing evening with some open mic performances, too. I ended up playing the guitar to "Blackbird" with a previous performer, Sofia.
