Having packed up James' fancy rented rucksack, and acquired last minute snack essentials, we headed onto the Kepler track to begin our multi-day hike.

A stunning walk through old beech forest next to Lake Te Anau, moss hanging off 40m
tall trees and so much green! A steady climb led us to some limestone bluffs with trees somehow clinging onto the rocks directly above us. Ascending some more we rose above the tree-line and into alpine wetland with spectacular views of the surrounding mountains and Lake Te Anau. The hut sat not much further along.

Visited Luxmore Cave after dropping our bags onto our bunk, we saw some huge formations of calcite!

Cosy evening in the hut, impressive facilities considering its location: flushing loos and gas stoves felt rather luxurious. James and I played spit (a two player card game) for a good while before making conversation with Maddy and two doctors from England who have moved to this side of the world.

Cooked up a rather delicious bean and rice meal. The envy of those picking at their sad, dehydrated foods was palpable. 

Ruth, the hut warden, delivered a wonderful
talk about the species of flora present in the beech forests, and what we could expect on the next sections of the walk.
