Skies cleared up in the early morn so packed up and got going by 9am. Solid 450m climb pretty much straightaway but with incredible views which kept me well motivated. 

At the top, the road opened up into a for droppingly beautiful valley with snow capped mountains either side, blue skies.

Rest of the ride continued to be incredibly scenic, if hilly, which demanded a second lunch.

Demolished a mega ice cream upon arriving in Wanaka and set up base at the Both Backpackers.

Went to see some live music at Cork bar with Taran.
