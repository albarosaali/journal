09:30 - left van to explore the Karangahake Window Walk. Led past/through old facilities and some mine-shafts; torch definitely required! 

Post mining, course charted for Opotiki, it serves us as a first base for a road trip along the eastern cape.

First stop made at Katikati where we obtained a bag of avocados for $1! Fresh water refilled. It transpires that Katikati is the avocado capital of NZ! 

Lunch break at Papamoa. Watched some
beginner surfers while we ate, I would like to try my hand soon! New glasses and mugs purchased at Op-shop.

Final stop Opotiki! Parked up next to a noisy neighbour, the sea. Pasta alla Norma for dinner.
