A surprise morning yoga session (yin style) led us to Hamilton East at 9:45am on a delightfully
sunny morning; much needed respite from yesterday's torrential storm.

90 minutes of stretching later, with supple muscles we explored the high street. MADE embraced us with its warm, modern glow, and we enjoyed some coffee and some excellent nibbles: the ginger crunch was a sweet and fiery explosion of flavour leaving one's mouth bathing in glory with each bite.
We returned again later after some successful re-stocking. Greek salad on the menu for me this time.

A speedy drive back to Raglan ensued so that I could make my final appointment of radial shock therapy at 16:00. Laughing helps with the pain rather a lot!

Butternut squash risotto for dinner.
