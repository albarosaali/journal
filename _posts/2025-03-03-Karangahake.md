Late 8am start. Quick phone home - scorching sun out. Pick-up of green certificate confirmed, we departed at 10am. Brief stop at Waipu for some extra op-shopping, then course set for Brown's Bay followed by the green-certificate. Lost out on a guitar on Trademe. 

Afternoon of driving towards Tauranga interspersed with a quick stop at Adventure Backpackers for post and at a bargain roadside fruit and veg shop!

Ended up just short of Tauranga at Karangahake Gorge which popped up at the side of the road. Used to be a gold-mining town. Noodles for dinner.
