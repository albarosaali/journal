A higgledy piggledy day with a hint of post-bbq lethargy.

Trip to town for a lunchtime coffee natter with Maddy, Anakayla and Shonie at The Shack - yet another café in Raglan.

Evening consisted of a frantic apple and tamarillo crumble making session so that we had pudding for dinner with Jonty. He prepared an ossobuco dish, rich and fantastically flavourful (secret ingredient, anchovies), demolished by all of us. He also shared a Gimlet Gravels Syrah  which no doubt contributed to the variety of dinner conversation delivered.
