Early start for kayaking around Doubtful Sound. Squeezed in a coffee before a safety briefing with our guide, Wolfy. 

The trip involved an extraordinarily scenic boat ride through Lake Manapouri. We soared between avatar-like mountains, shrouded in mist, before we reached the end where we caught a bus to the kayaks.

Tandem Kayaking is wonderful, especially in sparsely populated sounds! We soldiered through some strong headwinds for the first half, and were rewarded with sunshine and a tailwind for the second. Sheer mountain faces and rainforest surrounded us on all sides, captivating us with their magnificence. 

We arrived back in Te Anau, thoroughly tired and awed, though too had no time to  rest. We had to set about preparing for our hike, the Kepler Track!
