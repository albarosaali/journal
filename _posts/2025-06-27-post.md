Another bout of poor weather in Raglan meant a day spent mostly on the farm doing bits of inside work.

It had cleared slightly by the evening in time for round two of chai and kai. Jannis played at the open mic tonight with his band for about 1hr 45 mins! Good times. They even sang riptide with a little kid who sang his heart out. The evening then progressed onto a falafel wrap at the local kebab shop, surprisingly nutritious, a trip to the pub and then a house party at the band's house!
