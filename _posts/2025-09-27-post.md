Decided to sneak onto one of Philip's cave tours. Set off for an hour and a half at 10:15; certainly a cave experience I won't forget! Walked through water the whole way, and took in ancient stalagmites and stalactites inches from the eyes, a few had even reached all the way to each other! After a short crawling section, the cave opened right up and we had an opportunity to see glow-worms light up the inside in a beautiful constellation, a natural planetariun. Truly a magical cave.

Left the café at 13:00 and headed for Te Kauwhata. Arrived tired but without a hitch by 16:40: lots of brutal hills but the views were worth it! 

Went to watch the All Blacks vs Wallabies game at the local pub. Got talking to some locals. A well fought, very entertaining game! 

Tent pitched, bed-time!
