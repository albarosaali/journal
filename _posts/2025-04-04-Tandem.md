Early morning start. Quick tire pressure check before setting of on 3 hr expedition, to fetch the tandem I bought yesterday, to Taupuke!

3 hrs later arrived and met Anna. She was selling the tandem only because she - and her blind husband - had upgraded to an e-tandem, fancy! Took it for a quick spin and was immediately sold. Mounted, relatively securely, the trip back commenced.

Stopped for food and fuel on the way back, arrived in Raglan at 17:30.

Delicious dinner at Ulo's! Yuzu kingfish to start followed by an aburi sushi mix of salmon and scallop. The main, Kim Batu was superb, beautifully fried strips of pork belly with fresh vegetables and rice. The
stylish ambience adding to the taste experience with some funky beats by the DJ. Met three family doctors from Taranaki on a weekend away, another potential dog sitting lead.

Bed on James St again.
