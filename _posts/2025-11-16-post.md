Clear skies over the mountains, a morning treat! Took James to see the glacier. Majestic.

Slog of a journey up the west coast. Made a handful of stops along the way to Westport. In Hokitika we enjoyed fish and chips by the sea. In Greymouth we supped a few beer tasters on the Monteith's brewery tour with Millie, our guide. Pancake Rocks was our final stop of note: a fascinating geological formation of layered rock right on the sea.

Checked into Bazil's surf hostel in Westport. Noodles for dinner. Met Ellen who generously shared some fresh brownie with me.
