Leftover cannoli devoured at our final sea-front breakfast. 3 hrs passed asleep on the plane.

Just about had time to unpack and fix my mum's bike to make it for dinner with some friends at borough market. Elisabeth selected Agora: a new Greek tapas restaurant; after an aperitif Ouzo shot I knew we were in for a good time. Highlight's included the most succulent chicken thigh I've eaten in recent memory, a chard tzatziki: and some decadent pork belly. All this and more was polished down in the grand company of Elisabeth, Marius, Sam and Noel over rather more-ish red and white Greek wine. Lush!
