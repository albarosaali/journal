Sun decided to make an appearance this morning after a blustery night. Friendly man doing work for the department of conservation advised me that the route I was going to take was closed. I stuck to the highway.

Through headwind, I made it to Murchison: a small, nice town in the middle of nowhere. Stopped for a huge lamb shank pie and a conversation with a lady who warned me about inclement weather tomorrow.

Ended up another 30 odd kilometres down the road to set up base at Lyell campsite. Windy!
