Blessed with stunning morning sunshine at Tokomaru bay. Engine oil topped up; Vanessa will need a full change soon. Before leaving Tokomaru we purchased emergency chocolate in anticipation of a tough hike. Our final stop before starting our climb was Ruatoria where we sorted out our nights stay in the hut with the local - Ngati Porou - tribe, asked for any final advice and pre-cooked our dinner.

At 15:20 we left Vanessa at the car-park, next to a Waka (canoe), and began our ascent to the Mt. Hikurangi hut 1050m above sea-level. It was an 11 km slog uphill lightened by some jolly later sheep and spectacular views. Three and a half-hours later and somewhat windswept we arrived, the only guests in the hut. After admiring the interior graffiti and the magical views through the window, we quickly set about establishing ourselves: making the bed and starting a fire, the temperature dropping rapidly all the while. We ate, prepared our bags for the dark, sunrise ascent to the summit and went to sleep...
