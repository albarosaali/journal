Morning spent fencing and clearing, hacking away trees and annoying plants with a machete was particularly fun, if tiring.

Coffee purchased in town, pork too; ready for an Asado (firepit cooking) later.

Took Maddy to go and babysit Māhine, I ended up getting roped in to removing a bunch of solar panels. Another stunning sunset while I toiled.

Asado was fantastic. Nico made an excellent fire and swiftly created a cooking structure in equilibrium with a giant leg of lamb, which turned out deliciously. Marianne, a friend of the Germans, joined us and was very enthused about the lamb and the pork. In Nico's words "We made a good party!"
