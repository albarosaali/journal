Happy birthday Franci! A splendid lunch in Acitrezza was had to celebrate the occasion: a feast of seafood delights in an ampitheatre of coastal legend, the rocks which Polyphemus threw in angst at Ulysses breaking up the calm ocean before us.

The evening contained even more tasty delights: Miriam invited us all for dinner. She put up an incredible spread of pasta, arancini, caponata and fresh vegetables; everyone feasted! The standout for me however had to be the platter of enormous cannoli she heaved onto the table for pudding. They. Were. Divine!
