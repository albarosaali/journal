Tent packed up in the rain. Off by 10am. Mix of rain, followed by sun, followed by rain. Good chat with a random guy at Knight's Lookout. 

Eventually made it to Haast, desperate for lunch, and 45 km from my destination. Ate well, sipped a half beer and made tracks.

Arrived at the campsite soaked, in pissing rain. At least there was a shelter and two friendly blokes to chat to.
