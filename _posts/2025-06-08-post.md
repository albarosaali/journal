Morning spent tending to the farm animals before a joint decision was made that the weather was perfect for a hike up Mt. Karioi! We've been meaning to do it for the past two months! 

Started for the summit 11:30ish. Walk was short and quite steep, lots of forest which provided shade, preventing overheating. Reached summit by 13:15, 756m above sea level. Far reaching views enjoyed over a snack on a comfy rock, clear as day! By 15:30 we were back on the farm.

Painting kept us busy in the arvo and we enjoyed some great fire-side conversations with Jannis and Julia tonight; turns out he was a monk for 3.5 months!
