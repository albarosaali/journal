Left sunny Napier and headed north. First stop was Taupō; took James to see the McDonalds aeroplane, and to Huka falls which somehow was more impressive the second time around.

Next stop, Wai-o-Tapu thermal wonderland near Rotorua. Incredible Geothermal landscapes and some incredibly green water! Visited Kerosene Creek afterwards for a warm dip in a natural hot pool.

Zoomed therough Rotorua and eventually arrived in Mt. Maunganui. Watched sunset at Pilot's Bay. Incredible dinner at Rice Rice Baby: a beautiful aromatic duck salad, yum!
