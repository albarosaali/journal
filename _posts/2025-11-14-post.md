Left the hut early doors to carry on hiking through the forest. James continued to suffer with his knee so we decided to get the bus back from Rainbow Reach car park an hour and a half in.

Back in Te Anau we enjoyed superb venison and plum pies before slogging it back to Wanaka in the car.

Every single backpackers in Wanaka and the surrounding areas was fully booked! We ended up staying in a rather nice airbnb and decided we had earned ourselves steaks, asparagus, red-wine and cheese.
