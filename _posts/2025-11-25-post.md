Beautiful morning in Raglan, took James for a cycle to Ngaranui beach, then went for brunch at Wyld. 

Cycled around town and grabbed some beers for the beach. Lounged at Ngaranui for a few hours with a couple of dips in the ocean. 

Some of the afternoon spent packing, James leaves tomorrow.

Iso bar beckoned in the evening where we enjoyed oysters and cocktails for starters, followed by a cab sav and slow cooked lamb
shoulder. Of course we continued onto dessert; we had a rhubarb crumble cake and a tarte tatin, accompanied by port and a "nutty sailor" a coffee based pudding drink. Delicious.
