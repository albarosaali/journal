Relaxed morning with oats. Hot shower at the local hostel. Very mid weather. Attempted surfing, failed, so job hunted instead. Otherwise a slow day until the evening...

Ulo's beckoned! The live music hooked us in: a trio playing some funkified jazz standards very enticingly. Ulo's is hard to describe, the decor is a neatly organised and stylised version of things you might find in your parent's attic alongside some groovy colour patterns that spur psychedelic thoughts. I enjoyed a double whiskey, Maddy a glass of bubbly. 

Noodles for dinner today.
