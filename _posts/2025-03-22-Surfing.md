Morning flurry of eating-oats-chores and preparing for
a day out after surfing.

With Bindi set up for a stake out in the van, we donned our wetsuits, and some last minute surcream. then carried our boards down to the beach to join the rest of the group for the lesson. Zenor was our, softly spoken, instructor, cool as a cucumber and with a keen focus on fundamentals he promised that he'd have us all standing up in no time at all. After some stretching and position practice we marched into the ocean at Ngaranui bay, boards at our sides. I nailed it, stood-up, on my first go! I was ecstatic and went back for more, and more, and more. Before I knew it, it was 14:30 and the end of the lesson, what a blast!

Rest of afternoon spent on the beach being tired, reading, and playing with Bindi. Sunny and warm as! 

Dinner a variation of last nights. Search for live music led us to the Harbour bar where a trio were playing classic covers. We of course had a boogie. Met Nick on English expat to Aus. Shirt complimented by two blokes, winning!
