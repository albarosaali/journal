O2:45 start to drive to the airport. Smooth sailing in Auckland (other than Maddy's bag being stubborn about leaving her for the hold, it got stuck) and we arrived in Sydney 3 hours later. 

In Sydney I had the tools in my hand luggage confiscated, which I found frustrating since I'd just flown with them there. On well! While waiting for the next flight - Sydney to Singapore - I passed the time trying to set up my Raspberry Pi using just an iPad. It terms out that you can't easily. Oh well! 

About 5 hours later we plopped ourselves back into some plane seats and flew on. On the 8 hour flight to Singapore some tasty plane wine was had while Pic Picots autobiography kept me good company. That man has lived a very full life! 

Stomping around Singapore Airport provided much needed leg stimulation and some light relief in the form odd polite airport buggies and an outdoor cactus garden.

Within 2 hours we were back on the plane ready to transition into tomorrow...
