Early start. Oats. Hot showers located and sought out in nearby Kaitaia, lush! Morning admin hour confirmed that we could in fact stay at Trounson campsite without becoming singed by the nearby 96 ha forest fire. UMF 15+ Manuka honey also purchased!

Long countryside drive down route 12 until Opononi jumped out in front of the windscreen! Town resides on the shore of a river month that opens out to Tasman sea; the name Opononi comes from a friendly dolphin that visited and played with the locals 1955-56, Opo. Lunch and a large beer thoroughly enjoyed we continued on our way.

Trounson reached around 17:45 with a free marrow picked up along the way to make up for the avocado lost to defenestration. Strange aura at the campsite: felt-like your every move was being watched by a disapproving parent. Beetroot curry, cards, and an unfruitful attempt at kiwi sighting - we heard one though - in the black of night later, it was bedtime. Goodnight!
