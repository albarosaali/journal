Set myself the task of repairing the puncture I collected on my front tyre while arriving in Whangamata. This, frustratingly, took all day! Things went smoothly until my pump broke, eventually fixed it with grease, got the tyre to seat properly with a bit of soap, only to find that this unstuck the adhesive on the puncture repair patches, so I had to repeat the whole process all over again!

Met some nice people that kept me sane with small talk while I fought this puncture. Of particular note were Vivian and Larry: two Americans on holiday who offered me lunch! We spent a long time talking US politics, funnily enough. 

Got myself out to the supermarket and out to the each. The Whangamata coastline is stunning!
