Set off nice and early, greeted by a blue sky day, get in!

10 km in I got my first puncture, the rear tyre. Took me an hour and a half to coax it back into holding pressure. Carried onto a beautiful beach where I changed the rear brake pads and got chatting to a nice fella on an e-bike.

10 km later the same tyre went again, I needed a spare inner tube I did not have. Spent far too long trying, and failing, to patch her up outside a petrol station, before surrendering and heading to the pub across the road.

I met crazy Danny at the pub who was kind enough to ship me back to Blenheim, Bing's motel, where I spent the night.
