Went back to Goldie's in the morning for some fresh crumpets and a game of bananagrams. 

On the Michelle's recommendation we set off on a road trip around the West Cape on the surf highway. Lovely high street found in Opunake. Plenty of beautiful views all the way. 

Ended up back on the seafront where I cooked a banging Gorgonzola and nduja pasta. Later we headed to the Snug where me indulged in some cocktails, courtesy of Alex the bartender,  before heading to an Irish bar for a boogie to some live music.
