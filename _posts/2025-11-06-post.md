Solid exit from Queenstown by 8:30am. Big food shop completed, first stop on today's roadie was Wanaka.

Found a cheap mattress and chairs to improve any car camping we might do, bought James a budget bike helmet then took him on the lakeside cycle to Albert Town, that I did the other day. Went for a cold dip On the way, lush!

Enjoyed a beer and my favourite pie of NZ so faar: a Chinese 5 spice pork belly Sanga's pie. Unbelievably delicious. James opted for the Kiwi breakfast pie, complete with hash brown on top!

Departed Wanaka to go and camp at Aoraki/Mt Cook: New Zealand's tallest mountain. Dramatic scenery along the way: flitting between lush greenery, bush, valleys, huge lakes, and snow capped mountains. 

Steak for dinner tonight enjoyed surrounded by silhouettes of mountains and stars.
