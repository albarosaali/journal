Despite a brief interlude, oats were once again the breakfast stalwart.

Morning spent discussing future plans: now that we're found somewhere to make a base at, all the seasonal work applications are getting back to us!

Power electronics lecture completed.

Called up in the afternoon to move some cows into a different field and to try and find some goats (they had hidden themselves), half-successful I suppose.

Jonas and Clara offered to sort dinner so we took ourselves for a late afternoon walk at 'Bridal Veil' falls. A drastic 40m plunge for a flow of unsuspecting water that collides with gusto into the pool below! 

Pancakes were the dinner-fare on account of some prolific egg-hatching by the chickens! 

Agreed to cook everyone dinner tomorrow. Glass of wine shared with Nicolas over his successful visa application.
