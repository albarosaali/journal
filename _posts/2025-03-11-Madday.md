Oats consumed, we departed from Wairoa for Lake Waikaremoana for a morning hike. Long, twisting gravel roads navigated to get there. Unfortunately we managed to bend our rear tyre cage while parking, courtesy of a hidden tree trunk - the spare tyre went in the van while we ratcheted the cage to the towbar.

Parking palaver behind us we commenced our hike, beautiful virgin rainforest all around us with promising glimpses of a spectacular view flitting in and out of the branches to our right. About an hour later we arrived at the trig point on a rocky ledge with a vertical drop down to the lake. Bread rolls in hand we admired the magnificent view in-between mouthfuls, visibility for miles across the lake and to distant hills in the cloak of that blue-ish hue.

Subsequently we departed for Napier - approx 2 hours allegedly. Three and a half hours later we arrived, at
18:30, fortunately dinner had been booked for 20:15 so we weren't in a rush. First stop, 'Market St' for a glass of bubbly aptly named "Haha" from Hawkes Bay; absolutely delicious! We swooped down the road to 'Central Fire Station' where we indulged in some delicious food: oysters, raw fish salad, succulent lamb, delicate fish, chocolate cake and a choux bun Tiramisu; of course we had wine too:
Alpha Domus 'Blanc des Blancs', Albariño, Syrah, Port and a sweet cab-sav each accompanying the intricate flavours of our dishes most delectably. The best 'van-life' meal yet, obviously. A walk along the beachfront back to the pump track car-park capped the end of the evening. Happy Birthday Maddy!
