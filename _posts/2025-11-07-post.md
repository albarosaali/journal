Early morning hike of the open section of the Hooker Valley track, and the Kea Lookout was a wonderful treat for the senses.

Breakfasted in Twizek then zoomed to Lake Tekapo. Rented some paddleboards for an hour to go and explore the lake and the views.

Lunched in the arms of the mountains before making tracks for Opuke thermal pools in Methven. We left the pools extremely relaxed, found Big Tree Lodge where we could sleep and toured the blue pub, the brown pub, and the green pub. 

We gorged on wine and cheese for dinner tonight: on our way to Methven, we discovered an excellent cheese shop, in Geraldine. On the menu was, goat Parmesan, Waihi blue, 7 year aged cheddar, and Te Moana Brie, all absolutely delicious! The Pinot noir from Nelson provided superb company too.

Picked Harlan's (the host) brains for good tracks on the south island. He recommends the avalanche track in Arthur's Pass to Crow hut. One for the future!
