Up bright and early to go and catch some salmon! Visited Te Waikopurupu Springs on the way, probably the clearest water I've ever seen; the springs almost glowed blue in the rain!

Back to salmon. We borrowed some rods, cast, re-cast, casted some more,
and casted again until, finally, a salmon took the line. The fella was as strong as anything! I couldn't quite believe how hard he could tug, with James on the net we got him onto land, he was huge! 2.75 kg! I had half of him turned into sashimi for instant brunch consumption and the other half filleted for future meals. James caught another salmon not long after, another large one: 2.2 kg, which we had smoked.

Half a kilo of sashimi each later, we drove on to Blenheim in search of sunnier weather. Made a brief stop in Havelock to pick up some green-lipped mussels.

Chilled out evening in Blenheim, salmon for dinner, in plenty of butter, garlic and lemon. Yum!
