Woke up to mountains being kissed by golden morning sun. Ducks stole my first attempt at breakfast, pesky things!

Picked up some extra bits to improve car camping and set off to the airport to pick up James, he's here!

My plan was to tire him out as much as possible to chase away the jet lag, so we went up Ben Lomond (the tallest hikeable mountain in Queenstown). A tough walk with incredible views!

Enjoyed some beers on a sunny rooftop at the Local, enjoyed some huge ice creams, then set our eyes on a lamb steak dinner at Adventure Hostel. Yum!

Hostel beer pong beckoned, so we met a few people; James' team won! Result!
