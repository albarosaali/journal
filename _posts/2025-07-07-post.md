A relaxed day culminating in a golden hour hike up the Tirohonga track at Mt Pirongia. Not another soul seen until we reached the top where me met a bloke eating a sandwich. He gave us advice for a longer hike on the mountain for another day and pointed out what all the surrounding mountain ranges were!

We carried on a little further up, with some careful footing needed on chair sections, to get an even better view before heading back down. Lush.
