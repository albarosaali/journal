In van by 9:00 to make tracks for the Hakaramata trail, bacon baps packed as a tasty snack. Arrived by 10:00. Stormed up bush and steps; reached top by 11:20. Rewarding views of the Waikato river snaking through settlements. An eternity of stairs later we arrived back at the van for over baps. 

Afternoon spent acquiring hardware in Hamilton. 

Fixed the free salt lamp!
