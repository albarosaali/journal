A rather difficult start, following last nights festivities, but me made it onto the family coach at 8:15 and promptly set off to the harbour where the boat awaited.

Truly a blissful day on a magnificent vessel (it transpired that papa had pulled a few Nunziatella strings). Fresh tuna and swordfish kept is invigorated as we lapped up the Sicilian waves, played 'La Briscola', and jumped off the ship in every suitable place. We even managed a few off the stern! 

After an arancini and pizza dinner on the ship at sunset, we disembarked and made the return journey. What a day!
