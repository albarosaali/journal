Slow morning pack-up after a sunrise coffee. 

Left Kaiaua at 10am and sped off along the Hauraki Rail Trail towards Thames. I was initially blessed with a strong tailwind, though after kilometre 30, this turned into a sideways gale which proved incredibly annoying!

Visited "Bugger Café" themed rather humorously on "bugger moments" with plenty of wall art examples in case you needed reminding! 

Made it to Thames at around 14:00 yet to sort my accommodation for the night. Donated some clothes, met Justin who gave me some bike packing tips, and went to Café Melbourne for a smoothie.

Elected to go another 20km to the Totara Flats campsite and got absolutely drenched
doing so. The rain had, at least, the courtesy to stop while I put up my tent. 

More warming chicken noodles for dinner, with an avocado twist tonight!
