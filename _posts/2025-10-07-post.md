Poured with rain this morning! Managed to
hide my tent under shelter so at least it packed up dry.

Puncture repair didn't hold up so I replaced with a new tube and eventually I set off. The camp lady convinced me to head via the coast to Athenree they have mineral hot pools! 

Unfortunately for my legs, the way was beset with the worst headwind I have ever encountered: I could not accelerate down hill and was constantly fighting to keep the bike on the road. My only respite was served in the form of, you did guessed it! Another puncture! Luckily it coincided with a call I had scheduled about a solar powered hot-house in Raglan. Could be an interesting project!

Knackered, I made it to Athenree and made a beeline for the hot pools. Relief!

Camped next to some Germans on e-bikes and was rather envious.
