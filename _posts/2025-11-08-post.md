Decided to drive through to Christchurch this morning for a cycle along the coastal path. We parked by a black sand beach (Scarborough) near Sumner, and relaxededly rode the 10 km to the city centre.

We stopped briefly on the way, at a farmers market where I bought four small honeys to taste. When we arrived in the city centre, we happened almost immediately on a wonderful indoor food and drink market, down a small alley: the Riverside Market. Pies, anchovies and a beer supped for lunch. Pottered around the city and found New Regent Street; we enjoy ice cream in the sun there before cycling back to the car.

Brief dip in the ocean prior to leaving, then drove 3 hours down to Oamaru along some rather scenic roads.

Oamaru has a beautiful quarter with pristine Victorian architecture, it is very pretty!
