Incredible weather in Anglesey today, a rarity according to the
Tobin family but much appreciated.

A morning run to Rhoscolyn and then to Silver Bay kept us energised for teas and coffees at the lodge. Subsequently we prepared the kayaks for boarding and went on a romp around the headlands.

I caught a mackerel!

Beach ice-cream, a trip to the pub and a delicious tuna and anchovy nicoise salad rounded up a beautiful day: Marvellous.
