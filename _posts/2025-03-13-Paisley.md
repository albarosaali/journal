A resplendent sunrise over the pump track this morning.

Promenade walk up to library for an admin and planning bash; job application sent for chestnut picking.

Discovered excellent guitar shop owned by a very enthusiastic Liverpudlian called Richie who set it up 32 years ago; after some deliberation over lunch I bought myself a new acoustic! 

Maddy joined the Macpac puffer jacket club. In celebration of our purchases we visited Shed Five Thirty for a wine tasting and some fried bread. We met a lovely lady called Jennifer who put in a good word for us her boss and us up nicely for our Woolshed and Reserve wine tasting flights. We left with a bottle of the cheeky Woolshed Red blend.
 

Quick turnaround on an egg fried rice before heading to 'Paisley Stage' on Richie's recommendation to see some live music. What a venue! Trinkets and music art everywhere; the band played lots of classic sing-along covers. Richie bought me a drink and introduced us to his wife, cousin and the owners! The night ended with a guarantee that he'd be able to find us jobs easily, smashing!
Happy Birthday Eryl!
