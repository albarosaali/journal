Morning walk around the free geothermal attractions in Rotorua, including (smelly) Sulphur Point and a footbath in Kuirau park.

Lots of trip planning in downtime today and a little food shop before hopping on a bus to Te Pā Tū: a Māori cultural experience. We swung some sticks around and learnt some haka moves before settling in for a show of the real thing! A thrilling performance.

Later in the eve, we went for a dinner at the meeting house, where I made conversation with strangers, and are my weight in Wagyu beef.
