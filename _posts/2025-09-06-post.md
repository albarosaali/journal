Finally convinced the family to go on a hike! 

Led the pack from Newport Harbour, around Dinas head, and finished at "The Old Sailors" a locals pub with a majestic view of the ocean, nestled between cliffs. This trip, of course, was not without a lunchtime stop and swim. Pies enjoyed in the company of placid waves.

Papa and I left the others at the pub to walk all the way back. We very much enjoyed our march, and relished in the raw beauty of the scenery, the other way around this time.

We made it back to the Golden Lion at 19:45 to enjoy dinner with the others. I ate a whole plaice. Niccolo and I stayed later to enjoy a varied acoustic set - to put it lightly.
