Early start to catch the ferry over to Wellington. Passed the time catching up on this blog! We arrived by 11:30. 

Delicious pie consumed at Salut Bakery: Korean style pork belly. Succulent filling and perfect pastry. Sated, we headed to Te Papa, New Zealand's national museum for some cultural enrichment.

The first exhibition was rather somber, detailing the battle of Gallipoli in which the ANZACS fought like hell but ultimately failed to capture the territory, all at huge Herman cost, and in dire conditions.

The rest of the museum taught us about the founding of New Zealand, the unique wildlife, flora, and geology indigenous to this land.

Later on we drove up to Napier for a nights stay in the Art House Backpackers. Has some beers at Rosies in town.
