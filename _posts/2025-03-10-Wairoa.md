Beautiful sunrise at Kaiti beach. Oats again! 

Trip to laundromat, much needed. Town errands completed in the meantime. Laundry collected and assembled on clothes rack in van to dry.

New T-shirt acquired.

Lunch at Midway beach, beetroot salad and burnt neck. Attempted trip to a bank turned into a gander around the remaining op-shops, and an auction house in Gisborne. Subsequently ripped off for rubbish disposal :( 

1.5 hour drive along rural, winding roads to Wairoa, camping by river mouth. Solution for boot opening from inside devised. More daal and flatbreads for dinner.
