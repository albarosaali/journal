Morning chat with Tom about head injuries: he had to retire his pro-rugby career due to a particularly bad one.

Pedalled through rain to arrive in time for my tour of Pic's peanut butter factory. Excellent interactive tour and came out of it with a jar of peanut butter and a contact in Melbourne! 

Many kilometres later, I ended up in Tapawera where I enjoyed a venison pie and coca cola in the sunshine.

60 km later, through buffeting headwind, I made it to Kawatiri campsite, set up camp, ate, and crashed out.
