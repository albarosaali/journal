Left The Empire Hotel in Oamaru in search of penguins. Not sure if we did see any, but we did cross paths with a farmers market and specifically a delicious bacon butty. 

Stopped for a mutton pie in Palmerston, then zoomed along the 85 until we hit the Central Otago wine region. Pretty wild west sort of towns along the way.

Ruru wines was over first stop; we enjoyed a five wine flight in searing sun The sparkling and the Gewürztraminer went down the best, very refreshing. Our second, and final, stop was Dunstan wines where we met Mark is his beautiful home. We spoke about pretty, much everything except wine and thoroughly enjoyed his company, while we made it through five more delicious wines. We left with a bottle of his Riesling that left a particularly good impression.

Checked back into The Bothy in Wanaka. Comprehensively dominated the pool table at the Water Bar, leaving James rather bamboozled.
