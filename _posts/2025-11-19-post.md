The sun invited us outside ripe and early to get started on our cycling tour of the Marlborough wine region. Bikes unloaded, we got pedalling.

First stop, Wither Hills Vineyard. An interesting presentation of our tasters: they came in small vials, however the wines themselves, especially the whites, were superb. Left with a bottle of the chardonnay for safe-keeping. 

Our second stop, Framingham wines, was set up nicely though, the wines were not to our taste. We swiftly moved onto the third, Forrest Wines. The best presentation of the day complimented a varying and rather tasty selection of wines. The Albariño and rosé
(Pinot Noir and Malbec) particularly impressed. Here we also had a soothing midday gelato.

Gibson bridge was too busy so we tried Wairau River, the standout was a freshly bottled Pinot noir. To satisfy a craving for bubbly, No1 vineyard welcomed us with open arms. A delightful selection of New Zealand sparkling wine, James left with the Cuvée. While we were there, we met Kylie and Christie, two American girls touring NZ in an RV. Together we enjoyed some cocktails at the gin shack, and a pizza out at The Fancy Cow (named after Fanta, a highland cow on the premises).

We zoomed back on the bikes, James gratefully held my wheel for a tow, and crashed out. A fantastically huge day!
