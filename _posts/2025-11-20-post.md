Smooth departure from Blenheim by around 10 am to make tracks for Kaikoura. Scenic drive through fields of vines followed by a dramatic coastal road with jagged rocks abound in light blue ocean.

Soon enough we made it to Kaikoura where we explored the town, had one of the best pies of our trip so far (brisket, jalapeño and cheese), an ice cream, and to top it off went for a dip. The Pacific was freezing!

We set off to go whale-watching at two in the afternoon. An absolutely magical experience. We had a humpback whale breach not 5 metres from the boat! Dusky dolphins were somersaulting all over the place, Albatross taking off and flexing their huge wings, seals floating about lazily, and to top it off, a sperm whale! Super lucky!

Rather awe-stricken we made our way back up the island to Picton - our ferry is tomorrow morning.

Stayed at Atlantis backpackers. Met Alicé and Alex, and played pool with Rees and Joel. Also lost badly at chess.
