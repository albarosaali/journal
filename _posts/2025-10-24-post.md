Reluctantly departed my luxury accommodation, enjoyed a wonderful breakfast (cooked by Chontel), and set off for Greymouth. Tolerable wind for the first 30 km became a headwind for the remainder.

Venison pie enjoyed at Ikamatua, before crunching more distance through lots of old gold towns. Made it to Greymouth in good time, checked in to Global Village Backpackers, which is fantastically colourful and exotically decorated; then went to Monteith's brewery for a salubrious beer.

Met Aeron, Imogen, and Finn back at the hostel; fetched a magnum of red to share, and enjoyed each others company late into the evening. Played many games of Dutch Blitz: a fast-paced card game which is best learnt by practice! Wonderful stuff.
