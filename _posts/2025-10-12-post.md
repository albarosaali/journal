A rather misty day which soon evolved into rain. A brutal ascent occupied most of the first half of the ride, which got continuously muddier. I had to navigate  over 5 fallen trees in the middle of the track, great fun!

Eventually had the chance to descend which went well until... Snap! My rear pannier rack decided to shear on both sides. Managed to repair with some electrical tape, phew! Repair job also included a brief interlude of scrambling down a forested hill to retrieve my pesky sleeping bag which decided to roll down.

By 16:00, behind schedule, I arrived at the Timber Trail Lodge which I had been
assured served beers (according to some other riders on the trail). I opted for a coke and sat by the fireplace for an hour while getting to know Richard and Christie, who emigrated to NZ fifteen years ago!

Decided to continue on as far as I could in the rain. Small rivers flowed along the track as I sauntered on through what felt like more of a Jungle than a forest.

Eventually, getting towards 20:00, I found a random hut, with the added bonus of a door, which I chose to camp in.

Did I mention I decked it at least 5 times in the mud?
