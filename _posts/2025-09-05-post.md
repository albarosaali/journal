A slow morning. Enjoyed mushrooms on toast at Blas. Celebrated Mum and Papa's wedding 
anniversary by heading to Cardigan: a bustling artsy town just north of Newport. They had put on a fantastic arts and crafts fair with some seriously impressive pieces of pottery and prints; in the grounds of an almost 1000 year old castle! 

In the evening we went on a boat tour of the estuary with Dewi, who provided witty and interesting commentary throughout, while we enjoyed the scenery. We took his pub recommendation for a round before heading to dinner at the Printworks.

The Printworks is a fashionably decorated 'English tapas' restaurant with some service quirks. The food was nonetheless delicious with the pickled encumber, chilli and mackerel drawing out the biggest "ooo" from me. The chorizo and seabass came a close second!
