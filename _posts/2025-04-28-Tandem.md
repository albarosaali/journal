Cooked up a morning coffee for Elinor and a good morning of chin-wagging ensued.

Goodbyes made, Maddy and I drove on to Kinloch to take the tandem on some of the 'Great Lake' trails around Lake Taupō. After a small crash we decided to carry on in the opposite direction from which we started - much less sloshy mud this way! Great fun, though me may need to buy tires with more tread if we plan to go off-road again.

Slog of a drive home to the avo-farm, noodles for dinner and a hot shower before bed.
