Decided to cycle a long way today: from Taumarani to Ruatiti Domain (where I camped for free) A varied ~100 km route which took me on the road to Owhango, where I had lunch and a lemonade scone, then via dramatic cliffs along the Whanganui River to Blue Duck Station. 

The highlight of the ride was of course the Mountains 2 sea trail. It meandered through lush green forest, full of ferns. I saw goats, a whio (rare blue duck), and a spotted piglet. Met a farmer who owned some of the land on the trail.

Got drenched on account of the fact that I managed to lose my raincoat on the trail somehow. Anyhow, still made it to the camp before my bike light ran out of battery, phew!
