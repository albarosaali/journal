Morning switchboard wiring before a shift at Cala. Not very busy.

Quick-afternoon turnaround: tandem mounted to the van, bags and food packed, we were ready to go by 16:00. First stop made in Hamilton for the new front wheel from Franc. Second stop Mangakino, where we set up camp in... you guessed it? A car park.

Maddy prepared a three-course meal a la van
for our degustation including a boozy and delicious waffle and stout sauce dessert. A rich and robust Malbec, "Q", provided great company throughout.
