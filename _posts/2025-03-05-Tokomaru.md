Having enjoyed another morning of oats we began our journey along the East coast! Stunning driving through Lord of the Rings-esque backdrops all day, the highlight being the coastal road to the East Cape lighthouse.
Other memorable moments from today include a hike up Whanarau bay ridge - very much a bush hike: mostly overgrown and plenty of route guesswork - which some friendly dog walkers directed us to; some delicious macadamia nuts (oil also purchased); Waihau bay and a phone call with the local tribe to arrange permission to like up Mt. Hikurangi.

Camping overnight in Tokomanu Bay. Watermelon salad for dinner. Rain! So. Much. Rain!
