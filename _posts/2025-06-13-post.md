Early morning spent chasing an escaped cow, belonging to next door, around the paddocks! Bindi arrived at 9:30, cleaner and less sweaty than us; good going for a dog.

Took her on a new walk along the Wainui stream, very well behaved, even during the freestyle bush-whacking section.

Maddy made a corker of a warm lunch with sweet date surprises hidden inbetween the peppers, tomatoes and onions.

Mostly relaxing afternoon until I tried to put some more fence posts in and failed miserably, oh well.

Chicken thigh stew for dinner, yum!
