Arose this morning, applied for a job, then took my shovel down to the beach. I was greeted by a comical sight of people frantically digging to find hot water and create their own tub! Managed to make my own and blimey was it hot. A lot of fun!

Stopped at Tairua for a fried fish and avocado lunch. Met Michael, he offered some tips on how to get down to Rotorua and about free camping! Headed on to Whangamata, over more brutal hills, and set up camp by 18:30. Knackered. The water pressure in the shower almost knocked me off my feet! Not far off a water cannon!
