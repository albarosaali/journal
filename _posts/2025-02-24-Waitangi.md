Today we ate our oats and departed Otamure heading for Waitangi. We made a brief stop in Kawakawa where a train chugged down the middle of the high street, and we refilled our water. 

Waitangi is a recognised gathering space of iwi for discussion and debate. It is also where New Zealand's declaration of independence (1835) and the Treaty of Waitangi (1840) were ratified and signed; the museum provided a great insight into the colonial history of the nation. We also got to see an enormous, 3 tonne ceremonial war canoe constructed from three kauri trees!

Pork belly with rice and pak choi tonight. Camp made in the Hundertwasser toilet car park in Kawakawa; evening entertainment served by moaning mammals of some description in an adjacent field.
