Trench finished. Fencing. Massive avocado for lunch. Nap. Walk round wharf, muddy feet. Chocolate cookie from Maddy. Breathwork and dance at Ngarunui beach with Nick and Henry as the sun set - uber hippy! Easily the most incredible sunset yet: rolling waves as far as the eye could see reflecting a sky of myriad oranges and blues.

Dinner: butternut squash daal cooked for everybody and enjoyed around a fire.
