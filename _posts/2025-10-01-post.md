Woke up refreshed, even managing to lie in in my tent!

Headed back to Thames and stopped at Belle Café to recharge, plan and drink a delicious hot chocolate.

Bought lamb liver and other supplies; had a chat with a lady outside pak'n'save she gave me a book of inspirational quotes from the bible; set off for Tapu.

Arrived early evening, pitched up and started cooking a lamb liver curry for dinner, right by the beach. It turned out surprisingly well! Must make again.
