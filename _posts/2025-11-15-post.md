Mega breakfast treat this morning: bacon, eggs, avocado and sourdough. Yum! Feeling replenished we commenced our west coast roadie; back the way I had come on the bike.

Stopped at a 23 metre waterfall on the Haast pass, pretty stunning. Steamed on until Fox Glacier where we cycled to a view point through temperate rainforest. Cycling through rainforest is such a strange experience. The weather sadly did not provide a good view. 

Checked back into the Glow Worm hostel, still my favourite in NZ so far. Made use of the hot tub this time! 

Had some happy hour beers, some red wine and cheese, and later on happy hour
tequila and whiskey. Silly.
