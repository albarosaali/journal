A slow morning led into a rather delightful day of cafe hopping in warm sunshine. 

Stop 1: "Wannabe Spice" sandwich. A very fresh take on coronation chicken in a delicious focaccia. 

Stop 2: "Green Benny." Wyld café. Green hollandaise, a sweet potato hash and crackly kale invited an earthy sensation on the tongue. 

Stop 3: A "Hokey Pokey" ice cream for me! 

Stop 4: Early evening Lemonfish and chips at the wharf. Scrumptious!

Stop 5: Aperol spritz pit stop to admire the sunset. 

Very relaxed evening thereafter, enjoyed a final supper with Nice!
