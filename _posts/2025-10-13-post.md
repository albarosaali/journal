After decking it twice more, I got to the end of the trail and had breakfast and coffee. The guys at Epic cycles kindly let me wash my bike, and I set off for Taumarunui along gravel backroad which proved easy going.

The people at the Taumarmai i-site were very helpful with finding a place to stay. Unfortunately the weather turned brutally sour and punishing rain fell over the town. I arrived at the campground soaked but at least had a very hot shower to perk me up.
