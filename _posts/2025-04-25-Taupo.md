Picked up clothes from Nick at the physios in the morning, they should work! Used the opportunity in town to buy myself a new moka pot and some fresh coffee which made the van smell amazing. 

Set-off for Taupō before lunch; a rather scenic drive with lots of interesting rocks, a big dam and plenty of sheep.

Arrived at wedding venue, settled in then immediately set to work preparing cutlery.

Leftover ragú in the van for dinner.
