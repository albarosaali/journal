Oats of course, always delicious. Alternating morning runs with Bindi, my turn to relax, took morning opportunity to read the E+T magazine! Rest of the morning dedicated to van repairs: fixing broken seals with silicon, de-rusting various locations, attempted passenger seat repairs all completed, at least the first steps.

Noodles for lunch - we ran out of bread! Afternoon spent waiting for bits of the van to dry; playing guitar and reading.

About 1600 we set off for Ngaranui beach to take Bindi for a walk and to have a swim. Post extra running to fetch our forgotten sandals, we sat and watched the sun set with Anthony Keidis' drug binges providing good audiobook company. 

Broccoli pasta for tea, an Anne Stevenson Special!
