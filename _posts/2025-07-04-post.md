Sun un-expectedly arose this morning so we strapped the tandem to the back of the van and drove to Ngāruawāhia: the start of the trail. Beautiful, smooth surface almost the entire way along the river made a change from previous tandem adventures!

Made it 35 kms one-way (with a couple of un-planned detours) before deciding to head back. Of  course we stopped for some café snacks: a lamb and beef sausage roll; a cheese scone, and a coffee caramel cake all providing much needed fuel.

We made good time back despite getting caught in a torrential rainstorm which gave us a thorough soaking; this only added to the type 2 fun! 

Warming tagine whizzed up for dinner.
