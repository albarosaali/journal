Misty morning turned into a beautifully sunny day. Few hours of tandem repairs: front hydraulic disc brake was a little tight.

Set off from Mangakino at 11:00 to make our way to the Atamure dam. This essentially turned into another tandem mountain bike adventure, lots of switchback turns and steep ascents. Eventually arrived in Whakamaru where we stopped for a coffee before heading to the lakeside reserve for a spot of lunch. Chicken sandwiches and chocolate!

Zoomed back to the van and packed up quickly to make it in time for dinner in Cambridge. Maddy had booked us in at "Alpino", a swish Italian restaurant there. Starter cocktails accompanied the crumbed and feta stuffed olives and fish crudo; a Carabiniere for me, before a few glasses of red and our mains. We opted for a porchetta with salsa verde and fennel along with gnocchi and lamb "ragu". Tiramisu for pudding. I do miss Italian food!

Crashed out at our first paid for campsite for months!
