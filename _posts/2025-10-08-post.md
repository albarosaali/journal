Morning dip in the hot spring thoroughly enjoyed, I set off, leaving at 10:30. Of course it took me until 11:00 to realise I had left my towel, and trunks on the washing line, back at the camp.

I left the camp, for the second time at 11:30; path set for Mt. Maunganui. Thankfully there was much less wind today!

Stopped for lunch, and a pie, in Katikati. The place charmed me as much as the last time I passed through. Evidently, however, I did not fuel up enough as, less than two hours later, I crashed out at a petrol station where not less than two magnums, a Powerade, an espresso and two protein bars were consumed to revive myself.

Beautiful cycle path from Omokoroa to the Mount guided me the rest of the way. A local popped a few wheelies, and led me to the hostel, very friendly!

Spent the evening chatting and playing silly games. Lots of French people!
