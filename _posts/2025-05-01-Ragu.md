Morning of fixing power supply to our accommodation, we ran the batteries flat. Morning shopping run for tonight's Ragú. Began cooking at midday, meat was in by 13:00 (3 kg mix of park and beef, supplied supplied by Joy and Jonty) ready for a good 7-8 hours of cooking.

Applied for a job and visited the osteopath.

We all gathered up at Joy and Jonty's at around 19:30, they had put on a lovely starter spread of biltong, guacamole and wine among others nibbles which we enjoyed while employing all hands to roll the pasta and prepare some bruschetta. Everyone
enjoyed the food which was a relief and the ragú came out beautifully with the meat falling off the bone! Yum!
