Morning goodbyes at the Glow Worm, set off 9:30 in sunny weather and high spirits.

Tackled the three peaks between Franz and Fox without too much trouble, tiring of course though. Rewarded myself with a pie and chocolate bars at a café in Fox, where I struck up conversation with Matt and Monica, a Kiwi couple on a getaway, who had recently been to Europe. 

The headwinds were substantial today, but they did not break me. A brief ice cream at
Bruce Bay, boysenberry, helped me make the final push to Lake Paringa, a beautiful camping spot.

Had a lovely gnatter with Jamie and Jeff: the couple that I ran into in the snow on the way to the top of Alex Knob. 

Phone call with Ninai and Giusy in the eve, che meraviglioso!
