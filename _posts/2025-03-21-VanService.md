07:00 run with Bindi, well behaved, before taking the van in for a service at 08:30. Met Alistair, a very friendly mechanic, with whom we left the keys; the three of us then headed into town.

We bought yesterday's olive oil and rosemary bread from the bakery in anticipation for lunch, and proceeded to grab a picnic table by the pump track for breakfast - oats of course. Lots of ball for Bindi! Had a first crack on the pump track with the surf-skate, fell. Stayed here until lunch, long morning of keeping Bindi entertained and reading!

Delicious 'Hokey Pokey' ice cream in town, met Harry, one of Nick's friends, while eating it. Lunch consumed, followed by a delicious long black at Indi café; searched for bikes online. Butcher supplied bag of bones for Bindi in case we need a favour from her, as well as lamb for us!

Renewed van picked up at 16:00. Visited local growers market then  took Bindi to the beach to play more ball. Chill Friday eve back at the cabin: lots of guitar and reading. Dinner rather good tonight; seared lamb shoulder with tamarillo chutney on a bed of lentils, beetroot leaves, sugar-snap peas and ricotta, drizzled with balsamic glaze.
