Woke up rather early so resolved to climb the Mount. Great views enjoyed on a golden morning.

Out of the hostel by 10:00. Niels (who has bikepacked from Auckland to Bluff) wished me luck as I departed for Rotorua. 

A long, but pretty smooth ride today! Made it to a hostel in Rotorua by 18:00 and settled in a pub to do some research about the activities here. Rotorua is famous for its geothermal features and strong Māori culture.
