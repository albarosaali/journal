Woke up to rain. Packed up; hopped across the road to the café for shelter. After a coffee, two pies, and a chocolate bar, I convinced myself to brave the weather.

30 km later I arrived in Whataroa, cold and drenched. The rain felt like icicles piercing my face! Poured the water out of my socks and walked into a café for another coffee. Fortunately Donna and Martin (at a nearby table) saw me looking haggard and generously offered me a lift to Franz-Josef. I obliged immediately. They are a lovely pair; they dropped me off in the shelter of a forcourt saving me 30 km of further soaking. 

I ended up in "The Glow Worm" a charming accommodation Franz-josef with free soup, a fire and a hot tub. Made friends with others staying in the dorms, went to the pub together and I thereafter decided it was time for another red wine and cheese night! We had a great laugh together, a real pleasure to be in such fab company. Thank you Megumi, Vicki, Lucy, Ray, Taran, Brendan, John and Jojo!
