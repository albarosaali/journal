Today we hopped in the van early doors and made over way to "Fieldays": an agricultural exposition just south of Hamilton!

It would probably suffice to say that I've not seen so much farming equipment in one place before, ever! Highlights included: free (and delicious) barbecued sausages, the tractor pull, the digger operating contest, tasty treats in a food tent and of course meeting Kate (one of Maddy's friends she made in New Zealand the last time around).

Arrived back at 19:15, rather a long day. I prepared a salsicia é finocchio pasta for dinner. Scrumptious!
