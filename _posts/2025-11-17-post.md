Had a brief go on the hostel surf-skates this morning, before jumping back in the car and making our way further north. Along the way we took in some beautiful scenery, Buller Gorge. Picked up some obligatory pies in Murchison and cracked on.

Arrived in Takaka mid-afternoon and found a place to stay, Nirvana accommodation. Hopped on the bikes to go down to the beach but the tide was miles out, so we pedalled onward to see some more cool rocks.
