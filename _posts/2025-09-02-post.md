Morning gander to the nearby Parrog beach, a beautiful congregation of sea and land.

Breakfast of Blas café. Pottered around Newport town, a quaint town with an artisan touch and a fantastic butcher.

Niccolo, Papa and I decided we would summit a nearby hill: Mynydd Carningli and lunch at the top. We had a great view until we were engulfed by a large  cloud. Nonetheless, a good yomp. The Golden Lion beckoned and we supped a few post-walk pints to quench our perishing thirst. 

A delicious BBQ for dinner!
