Morning gallop with a noticeably lagging Bindi; we must have worn her out yesterday! Oats demolished.

Morning and afternoon spent sorting through finances, understanding why the fridge keeps beeping, getting inspiration from the Mezcla cookbook, and writing. While we busied ourselves Bindi did us the favour of rolling in a rotting carcass of a dead chick. The smell was gag-inducing, the kind that makes you wish that you had as developed a nostril as a fly. This event was all the more frustrating as we had just showered her that morning! 

Moving back into the van this evening. Took Bindi to play ball by the pump track. Watched the sun-set. 

Dinner: zucchini; lemon, garlic and tomato pasta.
