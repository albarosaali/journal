Big voyage today. Plenty of oats for fuel. Destination:
Cape Reinga - Te Rerenga Wairua - the Northernmost tip of New Zealand where spirits jump from this world to the next. Three and a half hour trip. Stopped for 'Killer' coffee on the way - Long Black. Astonishing views at the Cape. Mini pilgrimage to the lighthouse; sight of the Three King's Islands in the distance. This place is also where the Pacific and Tasman oceans meet turbulently in a whirlpool inducing dance; rather mesmerising to watch.

Next stop, the Giant Te Paki sand dunes. In an instant you are transported from ragged coastline to Sahara like desert! Sadly no bodyboarding, but the incredible view at the top of the dune was well worth the difficulty of walking up sand.

Evening encroached as we stopped for ice creams on the way to 90 mile beach - despite the enormity of mine I was envious of Maddy's Whittakers Peanut Choc Block. We made good time after, arriving just as Claudio and Matilde were leaving! It was an odd experience to be stood on a beach where more beach is all you can see. Some great shells and cheap road avocados found.

Stayed in Awanui car park. Tomato pasta for tea. Happy diamond wedding anniversary Nana and Grandad!
