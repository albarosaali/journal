Little lie in this morning before a sunny
stroll around Mt. Maunganui. Savoured a seafood pie for brunch then relaxed on the beach before our private surf lesson booked at 13:00. James' first time surfing!

Both of us out on 9ft longboards, good waves too. Managed to catch a green wave! With Bayley's instruction James was soon up on the whitewater, quick progress. A couple of hours later, and having received a good pounding from the waves, we were finished. Snacked on some pineapple by the beach to restore some energy into our bodies.

Set off for Raglan, arrived around 19:00 at our cabin in the holiday park. Enjoyed the No1 Cuveé and the St. Dunstans Riesling over dinner - tuna steaks. Met Aline. 

Went to the Harbour View Hotel for a few games of pool. I managed to pot 3 of my balls with 1 shot. This infuriated James.

Returned back and enjoyed the veiny blue cheese that we bought in Nelson.
