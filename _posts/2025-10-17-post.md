Decided to get out early (9am) and slog it to Wellington in one day. I arrived at midnight... 

Route took me mostly via motorway on a blue sky day. Finally got to see the snow-capped Mt. Ruapehu! Stopped for pie in Bulls, and a snack in Foxton. Even picked up some road honey, yum!

The trouble started in Levin. I picked up my first puncture. No matter, at least it was sunny! About 20 km later, I met a fatally surfaced bridge: it punctured both tyres! Punctures take me a while to fix as my pump is so small. At 19:20 I was back on the road, 73 km to go!

I imagine the views were great in the day, though of course it was dark by the time I got to the coast.

In the and, I made it to a homely Welsh pub in Wellington for a few, well earned, beers. Lush.
