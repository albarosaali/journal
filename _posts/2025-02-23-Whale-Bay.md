A luminous sunrise spurred us out of bed early doors
for a morning dip in Matapouri bay. Post bathing and subsequent consumption of oats we walked along coastal head to Whale Bay, a wonderfully secluded beach with tranquil waves perfect for Maddy's impromptu swimming lesson: she had forgotten how to.

For lunch we decided on 2 Hoki (fish) and 1.5 portions of chips for the princely sum of $20. Absolutely delicious - if a tad salty. Replenished, and simultaneously parched, we drove on to Otamure Bay for our next night of accommodation, with a shower too! 

There really are an abundance of incredible beaches in the Northlands! This one we blessed with a tomato and butter bean pasta.
