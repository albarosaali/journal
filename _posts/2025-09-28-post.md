Enjoyed a lovely sun-rise over breakfast before packing up and setting off by 10:00. Made smooth progress, navigated some curious cows and was feeling good until it transpired the map tried to take me through riverside bog. Nay chance! 

New route took me back the way I came. Stopped for lunch at Meremere, chatted to a friendly shopowner. Rest of the ride was scenic but gruelling, a corner shop popped up at the right time and I destroyed a chicken samosa alongside some protein bars and ice cream.

Relieved to arrive at Kaiaua at 16:30, finally. Sourced myself a beer and chicken; whizzed up some noodles and feasted! Met Mia, a friendly German lady, who's travelling up to the Northlands so I traded some recommendations for Coromandel tips!
