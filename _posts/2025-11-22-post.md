A refreshing steak and onion sandwich demolished for breakfast at the local farmer's market. This set us up wonderfully for our cycle ride.

Fully energised, we set off in search of our first cellar door. We got lost, so we had a pie and an ice-cream to nourish over navigational neurons. Voila! Tony Bish's cellar door was an all white, all Chardonnay affair, and delicious to boot. The founder had the wisdom to found a vineyard is order to fund a surfing lifestyle; seems to have worked rather well.

We got lost again searching for the next tasting so we headed back to the car and drove to two more. Mission Estate disappointed in the wine domain. Shed 530 provided us with a hefty pulled pork pizza, and a grand selection of wines in a wonderful setting. Got to throw a rugby ball there too! 

Later in the evening, we discovered that all the nightlife was on the other side of town at a rammed Irish pub and adjacent club. Had a mega boogie for the first time in a while.
