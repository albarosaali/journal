Second time lucky! 116 km covered today, with no punctures! This was, of course, possible due to cycle zone in Blenheim who discovered that my rear wheel had cracked, and had a new one for me, phew. 

Tried a 'bruffin' in Blenheim, pretty good, and stocked up on peanuts for the ride. Demolished a green mussel pie in Havelock after facing 40 km of gruelling headwind.

Had the best descent of the trip so far: beautiful bendy road down the Rai valley
in golden hour. I even kept up with traffic. 

Good conversation with Tom at Bridge Backpackers, also met Hiloki who had to dig his car free today with his bare hands! 

Got attacked by three birds, they just fly into my helmet for some reason. Incredibly annoying.
