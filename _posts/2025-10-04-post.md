Bought myself a large rib-eye steak for the evening. Crossed over the estuary, from
Whitianga, to make my way towards Hahei. A lovely sunny morning; I passed some beautiful beaches on the way!

Made it to Hahei, and it immediately started to rain. Classic. Eventually let up so I marched myself to Cathedral Cove, a beach with a stunning natural arch and beautiful views of archipelago.

After a chat with a friendly lady in an art shop I decided to spend the night at Hot Water Beach: I hadn't realised that you had to go at low tide! 

Spent evening in the pool and in the sun, before cooking and savouring my steak with a glass of red.
