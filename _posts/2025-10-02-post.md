Enjoyed a beautiful morning waking up by the sea, and managed to set off before 10! Another hot shower before leaving snuck in.

Smooth going until dreaded hills turned up. Two brutal inclines sapped my life-force, I just about kept myself propped up with nuts and raisins until I finally reached the outskirts of Coromandel Town.

A takeaway fish shop served as the ideal location for a late lunch. I had their fried oyster bao buns!

Set-up camp at Tui Lodge, recommended
by the i-site: Visited Coromandel Town Supply brewery where I tried all the beers and took some away in a spare bottle! Yummy!

Rode to Long Bay and walked to see a 1200 year old kauri tree. It was a magical walk with beautiful golden sun showing the flora off at its best. Lush!
