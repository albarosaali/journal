A rainy morning, made pleasant by the company of Lucy and Vicki over coffee at "Full of Beans", using the hostel's 10% discount.

Very helpful lady at the DOC centre recommended the "Alex Knob" hike for a good view of the glacier, and so after a heap of admin and an early lunch I set off.

Of course it hailed on me on the bike ride over (hail stings a surprising amount in shorts), no matter! The hike was spectacular: mossy rainforest evolved slowly into sparse mountain shrubbery revealing incredible views of coast and snowy peaks simultaneously. The last section had me trapsing through snow, my oh my was the view of the glacier worth it! After staying at the top, in awe of my surroundings, for a good 20 minutes and a chocolate bar, I marched all the way back buzzing. Took 5 hours in total. What a walk! 

Treated myself to two steaks, four eggs, an avocado, and a tomato for dinner. Fantastically, Vicki and Lucy decided to make red wine and cheese a double header event, woohoo! Alex and Jocelyn (new arrivals) joined in and introduced Cabot (a card game) to the table; another lovely evening, thanks all!
