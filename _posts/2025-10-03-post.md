Morning spent searching for jobs at Tui Lodge until it transpired that I had overstayed my welcome. Whoops!

Packed tent up in the rain and set off for Whitianga along the old gravel road. The beauty of the valley made up somewhat for another bugger of a hill, though at least there was only one today!

Made it the rest of the way in good time and settled in a cabin, drenched once again, at Mercury Bay holiday park. Bumped into a lovely couple who gave me some cycling reccys, they had done many of the great rides! 

Interesting chat with Zio Umberto about some huge solar projects, and reviewed Robins renewable hot-house proposal for Raglan.
