A heatwave struck us today, the sun was out in full, blue sky kit! This made using the auger sweaty work; we were building the foundations for Joy and Jonty's new cabin.

Fresh scones at 11:30 courtesy of Jonty, again delicious! Laboured on until 3pm. The final act, moving a sofa down to our accommodation.

The early finish meant we could go for a sunset surf and my, oh my was it a good one! Hoping for more sunny days like this!
