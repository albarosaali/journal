Sunshine this morning convinced me to take a detour: off the highway onto the West Coast Wilderness trail. A very nice ride through some lush scenery and along water races, so much gold around here!

Enjoyed my first date scone in "Cowboy Paradise", a sort of faux wild west high street in the hills. It had a fantastic view! 

Stopped in Hokatika and wished I could have stayed, a bustling little coastal town. Unfortunately no backpackers.

Ploughed through headwind to get to Lake Mahinapua campsite, the nicest one I've been to yet!

Had a nice crash on some loose gravel which has left me rather bruised.
