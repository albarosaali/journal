Rear solar panels installed with Flynn's help; sun was beating down on us. A very hot day! Prepared homemade burgers for the BBQ. Drove to Ruapuke beach to wish Gemma a happy birthday, and go for a swim. 

Huge BBQ evening with guests, Aline, River and Elliot came along. Spread of burgers, guacamole, peri-peri chicken, halloumi skewers, Greek salad, and of course, a few beers! Went swimmingly, minus Jonty nearly setting fire to the accommodation, oopsies.

Beat some more non-native English speakers at Bananagrams again, then went for a full moon surf at midnight with Bar, Flynn, and Elliot. A magical experience at Manu Bay, my first time surfing there. Nearly caught a few waves with tips from Bar, learnt a lot! Managed to rip a hole under my balls in my wets suit though, whoops. Got back and crashed out at 4am.
